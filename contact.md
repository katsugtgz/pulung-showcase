## 🛠️ Services & Facilities

* **Transmission Types:**
  * **Manual (MT):** Traditional manual gear shifting techniques.
  * **Matic (AT):** Automatic transmission driving.
  * **Mixed Package:** Dual-training course covering both MT and AT.
* **Vehicle Fleet:** Fully air-conditioned (Full AC) training units.
* **Instructors:** Certified, professionally trained, and emphasized as highly patient.
* **Motto:** *"Safe Drive Training"* – Focused on defensive driving, safe road practices, and building beginner confidence.

## ⏱️ Operation & Pricing

* **Working Hours:** Hours are flexible but not statically published. Live schedules must be coordinated directly with the respective cluster admin on WhatsApp.
* **Pricing Model:** Advertised as "affordable and competitive". 
  * Prices are dynamic (subject to promotional packages).
  * Direct WhatsApp inquiry is required to get the current pricelist sheet.
  * *Tip:* Ask about bundled 5-session or 10-session discount packages.

## 💡 Recommended Registration Questions

When reaching out to the admin, verify the following details:
1. **Package Promos:** Are there active promos for 5x, 8x, or 10x training slots?
2. **Scheduling Flexibility:** Do they offer weekend/after-hours slots, and is there an extra fee for those?
3. **SIM A Licensing:** Do they offer integrated packages that include administrative assistance for the Official SIM A (Driver's License) creation with the local Police?
